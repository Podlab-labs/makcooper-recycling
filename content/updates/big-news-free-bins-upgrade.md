♻️ **BIG NEWS! MAK & COOPER'S UNWANTED CANS & BOTTLES RECYCLING SERVICE IS GETTING AN UPGRADE!** ♻️

Hi everyone! 👋

We've been working hard behind the scenes to make our recycling service even easier and more sustainable for our amazing supporters.

We have been able to purchase some second hand bins that we have thoroughly cleaned ourselves, so we are now able to offer **FREE bins** to our regular supporters!

All you need to do is:

1. Pop your eligible cans and bottles into the bin as you collect them.
2. Send us a message when it's getting full.
3. We'll come and empty it, replace the liner if needed, and leave it ready for you to keep filling.

It's that simple!

✅ FREE bin for regular supporters.
✅ No sorting required.
✅ Easy and convenient.
✅ Helping keep recyclable containers out of landfill.

We really appreciate all the support that we have received. Every bottle and can you save for us helps us work towards our goals while making a positive difference for the environment. 💚

Our service is completely donation based. We don't pay cash for cans and bottles. Instead, we collect the eligible cans and bottles that you no longer want or would simply like to get rid of on a regular basis.
