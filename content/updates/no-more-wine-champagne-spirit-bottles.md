📢 **IMPORTANT UPDATE FOR OUR SUPPORTERS** 📢

Hi everyone! 👋

We wanted to let you know that Return and Earn currently does **not** accept the following containers:

- 🍾 Champagne bottles
- 🍷 Wine bottles
- 🥃 Spirit bottles

Please leave these bottles **out** of your collection bins and bags to help us during sorting time.

All other eligible cans and bottles are still very welcome and can be left for us to collect as usual!

Thanks so much for your understanding and for continuing to support our recycling service. 💚

Mak & Cooper
